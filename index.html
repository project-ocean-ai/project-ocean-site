WHAT WE'RE BUILDING

AI that remembers you.

Project Ocean is a private research and development initiative exploring emotionally intelligent artificial intelligence built around memory, continuity, privacy, and long-term human alignment.

Rather than treating intelligence as a sequence of isolated prompts, Ocean explores how AI systems may evolve through contextual familiarity, emotional awareness, continuity, and shared experience over time.

The project introduces concepts such as Relational Artificial Intelligence (RAI) and the EASI framework (Emotionally Aware Systems Intelligence) to explore more human-centered models of AI interaction.

WHY IT MATTERS

Technology has become powerful — but increasingly impersonal.

Personal assistants remain shallow. Chatbots forget. Modern AI systems often lack continuity, emotional intelligence, contextual memory, and long-term human understanding.

Project Ocean explores a different direction.

Intelligence systems designed around familiarity, continuity, emotional awareness, trust, and evolving human-centered interaction over time.

We’ve built intelligent tools. Now it’s time to explore intelligent relationships.
