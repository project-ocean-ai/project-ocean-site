Tonight was one of those moments where everything started feeling more coherent.

Not because of hype.
Not because of funding.
Not because of headlines.

Because the deeper I think about the future of AI, the more I believe the real challenge isn’t simply intelligence.

It’s trust.

Trust in memory.
Trust in privacy.
Trust in continuity.
Trust in systems that understand context without taking ownership of the person behind it.

I don’t think emotionally intelligent AI can exist without human-centered design being foundational from the beginning.

The future of AI will not belong only to the most powerful systems.

It will belong to the systems people actually trust.
